# 🎃 Midnight Munchies

A spooky, arcade-style Halloween game built entirely with **p5.js**. Play as a mischievous floating ghost collecting candy while dodging swooping bats and evil jack-o'-lanterns!

---

## 🎮 Features

* **Custom Monster & Graphics:** Hand-drawn vector-style ghosts, candies, flying bats, and jack-o'-lanterns.
* **Dynamic Difficulty:** The game speeds up and spawns hazards more frequently as your score increases.
* **Golden Candy Bonus:** Keep an eye out for rare, glowing golden candies worth extra points!
* **Particle Effects:** Custom particle bursts when collecting items or taking damage.
* **Smooth Controls:** Full support for Arrow Keys or W/A/S/D movement.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Objective:** Collect as much candy as possible without losing all 3 lives.
2. **Controls:**
   * **Move Up/Down/Left/Right:** `Arrow Keys` or `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`
   * **Start / Restart Game:** `Enter` or `Spacebar`
